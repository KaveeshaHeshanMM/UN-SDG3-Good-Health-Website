Stay Active & Stay Healthy: Your Guide to Fitness

6. Nutrition for Fitness

A healthy diet is essential for fueling your workouts:

  • Pre-Workout Snacks: Bananas, oatmeal, or a smoothie.
  • Post-Workout Meals: Protein-rich foods like chicken, eggs, or tofu, paired with carbs like rice or sweet potatoes.
  • Hydration: Drink plenty of water throughout the day.
