Stay Active & Stay Healthy: Your Guide to Fitness

1. Home Workouts

You don't need a gym to stay fit! Here are some effective home workout routines:

  • Beginner Routine:
    • 10-minute warm-up (jumping jacks, arm circles).
    • 3 sets of 10 squats, 10 push-ups, and 10 lunges.
    • 5-minute cool-down (stretching).
  • Intermediate Routine:
    • 15-minute HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training): 30 seconds of work, 10 seconds of rest.
    • Exercises: Burpees, mountain climbers, plank holds.
  • Advanced Routine:
    • 20-minute circuit training: 4 rounds of 10 pull-ups, 20 push-ups, 30 squats, and 40-second planks.
